# Immigration Horizons Content Guide

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Defines the content standards, writing style, SEO requirements, and publishing workflow for the Immigration Horizons website and CMS.

All website copy, service pages, blog posts, landing pages, and marketing content must follow this guide.

---

# Content Objectives

- Build trust and credibility
- Educate potential clients
- Generate qualified leads
- Improve organic search rankings
- Support EEAT principles
- Maintain consistent brand voice

# Brand Voice

Content should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Educational
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Confident

Avoid:

- Legal guarantees
- Misleading claims
- Fear-based marketing
- Keyword stuffing

---

# Writing Standards

Use:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear headings
- Bullet lists
- Actionable language
- Plain English

Every page should answer the user's question before promoting services.

---

# Service Page Structure

1. Introduction
2. Eligibility
3. Benefits
4. Requirements
5. Process
6. Required Documents
7. Why Choose Immigration Horizons
8. FAQs
9. Call to Action

---

# Blog Structure

Every article should include:

- H1 Title
- Introduction
- Table of Contents
- Main Sections
- FAQs
- Internal Links
- CTA

---

# SEO Requirements

Every page must include:

- SEO Title
- Meta Description
- Clean URL Slug
- Canonical URL
- Internal Links
- External Authority Links
- Image Alt Text
- Schema (where applicable)

---

# Keyword Strategy

Primary Topics

- EB-2 NIW
- EB-1A
- EB-1B
- EB-1C
- O-1 Visa
- USCIS
- US Immigration
- Green Card
- Petition Preparation
- RFE

Use one primary keyword and supporting related keywords naturally throughout the content.

---

# Internal Linking

Every page should link to:

- Related Services
- Relevant Blog Articles
- FAQs
- Contact Page
- Consultation Page

Avoid orphan pages.

---

# Media Guidelines

Use:

- High-quality images
- Icons
- Infographics
- Process diagrams
- Videos (where relevant)

Optimize all media for web performance.

---

# Compliance

Never:

- Guarantee approval
- Promise processing times
- Publish unverified statistics
- Present Immigration Horizons as a law firm if that is not accurate

Always distinguish consulting and petition preparation services from legal representation.

---

# CMS Requirements

Editors should manage:

- Drafts
- Scheduled Publishing
- Authors
- Categories
- Tags
- SEO Metadata
- Featured Images
- Related Articles

---

# Content Review Checklist

Before publishing:

- Grammar checked
- Facts verified
- SEO completed
- Internal links added
- Images optimized
- Schema configured
- CTA included
- Reviewed and approved

---

# Success Criteria

Every published page should be:

- Helpful
- Accurate
- SEO optimized
- Easy to read
- Consistent with the Immigration Horizons brand
- Designed to generate qualified consultation requests
